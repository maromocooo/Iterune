# Agent labels and artwork

Claude, Codex and Gemini are identified by their product names and original text
badges (`Cl`, `Cx`, `Ge`) drawn with SwiftUI. These badges are not official logos.
Their appearance follows the surrounding light/dark foreground style, and their
accessibility labels retain the full product names.

Vendor logo files are not included in the current resources or app bundle.
Redistribution permission for the previously included desktop-app and website
images was not established, so those images have been removed from the current
tree rather than treated as licensed artwork. This change does not remove images
from existing Git history or previously built releases.

Product names and trademarks belong to their respective owners. Identification
does not imply sponsorship or endorsement. The project's MIT license does not
grant rights to third-party trademarks or historical vendor artwork.

The Iterune application icon is separate, original project artwork
generated by `scripts/make-icon.swift`; it has not been replaced.
